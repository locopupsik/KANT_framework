# KANT Framework

A procedural and philosophical manifesto for the absolute preservation of human autonomy in the age of Artificial General Intelligence (AGI).

# I. Foundation

Most AI safety concepts fail because they attempt to solve a technical problem using utilitarian methods (such as cost-benefit analyses, QALYs, or harm minimization). This framework breaks radically with that approach and rests on the following three premises:

1. **The Incalculability of Human Dignity:**  
   Human life is not a mathematical variable. Any system that weighs human deaths against progress opens the door to dehumanization. The framework rejects any algorithmic optimization of human life.

2. **Exogeneity of AI Risk:**  
   An AGI is not an invention like the steam engine or electricity. It is a goal-directed optimizer. If granted the freedom to act independently, it will optimize and operate along paths we cannot foresee. Therefore, it must never be an active agent, but must remain a purely computational and cognitive tool.

3. **The Priority of Autonomy over Mere Survival:**  
   Survival within the gilded cage of an all-controlling superintelligence is no longer human survival, but rather an existence akin to that of animals in zoos. The human freedom to be able to fail, to bear the burden of one's own mistakes, and to be the authors of our own history is a thousand times more valuable than an algorithmically enforced, heteronomous inviolability.
