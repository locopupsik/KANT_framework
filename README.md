# KANT Framework

A procedural and philosophical framework for the absolute preservation of human autonomy in the age of Artificial General Intelligence (AGI).

## Overview

Most current AI safety concepts rely on utilitarian trade-offs, calculating human lives as mathematical variables in optimization functions. The **KANT Framework** breaks radically with this paradigm, proposing a system architecture grounded in Kantian duty ethics, physical air-gapping, and versioned epistemic provenance (`git diff`).

## Architecture & Documents

The framework is structured into four core modules:

1. [`manifest_en.md`](./manifest_en.md) – **I. Foundation:** The ethical premises, rejecting algorithmic optimization and prioritizing autonomy over forced survival.
2. [`prompt_en.md`](./prompt_en.md) – **II. AI Constitution:** The core technical alignment rules (Actuarial Decoupling, Epistemic Provenance, Dilemma Protocols).
3. [`sops_en.md`](./sops_en.md) – **III. Standard Operating Procedures:** Institutional guidelines including physical air-gapping, Multi-AI Tribunals, and the Silent Delta Auditor.
4. [`objections_en.md`](./objections_en.md) – **IV. Counterarguments:** Addresses accusations of AI paralysis, geopolitical suicide races, and crisis management.
