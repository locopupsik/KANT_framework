# III. Standard Operating Procedures: The SOPs

The strict rules for human institutions in dealing with the machine:

- **The Isolation Doctrine (Air-Gapping):**  
  The AI is physically separated from the Internet and physical infrastructure (air-gapping). It serves as a source of ideas and a calculator, never as an executor.

- **The Multi-AI Tribunal & Translation:**  
  Independent control AIs (trained differently) review the output and break it down into humanly comprehensible logic in order to intercept the cognitive barrier.

- **Iterative Micro-Deployment:**  
  The implementation of blueprints or systems occurs strictly in isolated sub-steps (sandbox → local field experiment) to rule out unforeseen butterfly effects before global scaling.

- **The Silent Delta Auditor (git diff):**  
  The AI never proactively intervenes in political processes; it neither warns nor raises the alarm. It merely generates, in the background, a bone-dry, value-neutral diff report that documents which variables were omitted in human summaries and how this affects model precision.

- **The Data Audit Office:**  
  An independent, democratically legitimized government institution staffed by humans, whose sole task is to review these diff reports, identify information losses, and, if necessary, sound the political alarm.
